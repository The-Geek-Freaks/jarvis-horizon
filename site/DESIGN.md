# Public demo surface

Extend the existing Horizon visual world: deep navy, warm gold, cool blue,
Space Grotesk, a detailed central orb and generous dark space. The actual
shipped renderer is the lead visual. Do not imitate it with a second UI.

Visitor path: understand the purpose, explore real frontend states with sample
data, choose Android or Pi installation, contribute a device report or setup.
English first; the website also has German copy. The embedded renderer retains
the German labels of the alpha. No account, audio capture, analytics, backend
connection or real home action in the demo.

Scene and format controls belong to this website, not to the device app.
Direct links preserve scene, format and language. Reduced-motion preference
and a visible motion toggle use a static renderer snapshot. Small screens
default to portrait. Controls are keyboard accessible and sample status stays
visible outside the display. Use actual shipped assets, no fake testimonials,
benchmarks, stars or connected services. Static Pages build uses an allowlist.
